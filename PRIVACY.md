# PhoneDrop Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 14 November 2026*

PhoneDrop ("the app", also published as "Send to iPhone") moves files and text between your Windows PC and your iPhone over your local Wi-Fi network.

## What the app collects

**Nothing.** PhoneDrop has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The developer never receives your files, your text, or any information about you or your devices.

## How your files travel

- Files and text go **directly between your PC and your iPhone over your local network**. They never pass through the internet or any server run by the developer.
- Files you send from your iPhone are saved on your PC in `Downloads\From iPhone`.
- Files you share from your PC stay where they are. PhoneDrop only lets your paired iPhone download them.
- Clipboard text you send is kept only in the app's memory and on the receiving device's clipboard.

## Data stored on your PC

PhoneDrop stores a small settings file on your PC. It holds:

- your preferences (for example, whether the app starts with Windows)
- the list of files you have shared
- a random pairing key

The pairing key is stored in Safari on your iPhone as a cookie, so only the iPhone that scanned your QR code can connect. Choose **New link** in the app at any time to revoke it.

## Microsoft Store

If you install PhoneDrop from the Microsoft Store, the app asks Windows whether your copy is on a trial or fully licensed. Microsoft handles this check and all purchases under the [Microsoft Privacy Statement](https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement). The developer does not receive your payment details.

## Children

PhoneDrop does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.
