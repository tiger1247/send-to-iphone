## PhoneDrop: product page

**Name:** PhoneDrop: send files between PC and iPhone

**Summary / tagline:** Wi-Fi file transfer for Windows ↔ iPhone. Nothing to install on the iPhone.

**Description:**

Move photos, videos and files between your Windows PC and your iPhone in seconds, over your own Wi-Fi. There's no app to install on the iPhone, no cable and no account.

**How it works**
Scan a QR code once with your iPhone camera. Safari becomes your transfer page, and you can add it to your Home Screen.

- **PC → iPhone:** drag files onto PhoneDrop, or right-click any file and choose *Send to iPhone*
- **iPhone → PC:** pick photos, videos or files on the phone, and they land in *Downloads\From iPhone*
- **Shared clipboard:** send a link or some text either way
- Full-quality videos with progress bars, and folders sent as a .zip
- Starts with Windows and lives in the tray

**Private by design:** files travel only over your local network. They never touch the internet, and there are no ads or tracking.

**What you get:** a Windows 10/11 download (28 MB) with a one-click installer and free updates for version 1.x.

**Requirements:** Windows 10 or 11, and a PC and iPhone (or iPad) on the same Wi-Fi.

*Note: PhoneDrop is a small indie app and isn't code-signed yet. On first run Windows may say "Windows protected your PC". Click **More info → Run anyway**. If your PC has **Smart App Control** turned on, get PhoneDrop from the Microsoft Store instead (search "PhoneDrop").*

**Thank-you / receipt message:** Thanks for buying PhoneDrop! Extract the zip, then double-click **Install PhoneDrop.cmd** and scan the QR code with your iPhone. Need help? https://github.com/tiger1247/send-to-iphone/issues

## InkNote: product page (pay what you want)

**Name:** InkNote: pen-first notebook for Windows

**Summary / tagline:** Handwrite, highlight and draw on paper-like pages. Free, and pay what you want to support it.

**Description:**

InkNote is a fast, pen-first notebook for Windows that feels like writing on paper. **It's free.** If you like it, you can pay what you want to support development.

- Smooth, pressure-sensitive ink for Surface pens, XP-Pen and Wacom tablets, mouse and touch
- Pens, pencil and highlighters in your own colours; hold the pen still to straighten a line
- Lasso to move, resize, recolour and copy; text boxes, pictures and shapes
- Ruled, grid, dotted or plain paper in 11 colours; light and dark themes
- Subjects and pages with search
- Export a page, a subject or everything to vector PDF (A4, Letter or one long page)
- Auto-save with daily backups; works offline with no account

**What you get:** a Windows 10/11 download (115 MB) with a one-click installer.

*InkNote is also free on the Microsoft Store ("InkNote - Pen Notebook"), and the source is on GitHub: https://github.com/tiger1247/inknote. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click **More info → Run anyway**.*

**Thank-you message:** Thank you for supporting InkNote! Extract the zip and double-click **Install InkNote.cmd**. Your notes are saved in Documents\InkNote.
